An entry for make.fun's "Till Firmware Do Us Part" bounty. The program writes one wedding vow to `/output/entry.txt`: a robot at its own wedding promises to love its partner in full signal and in airplane mode, through every update, every crash, and every low battery, until its final shutdown. The vow is a machine's "till death do us part" — firmware, signal, and battery standing in for sickness, health, and forever.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the vow (164 characters, one line) to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).
